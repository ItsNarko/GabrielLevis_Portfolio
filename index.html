02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I’m still
figuring things out.
And that’s okay.

Curiosity is the common thread.

First and foremost, I see myself as a student. There’s always more to learn, and I don’t think that stops when a course ends.

I’m studying IT Programming at NSCC COGS, but my interests have never really stayed in one area. I enjoy coding, print and digital design, and photography. I like having different ways to approach an idea, whether that means building something that works or making something worth looking at.

This portfolio brings a few of those interests together. Some of the work comes from college, some comes from exploring things on my own. It’s a reflection of where I am right now, with room to keep growing.

I also work with the Association francophone de la vallée. My roles bring together technology, community activities, and a newer focus on environmental education. I communicate in both French and English.
